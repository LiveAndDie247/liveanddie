TOOLS
Free browser tools made in-house. Nothing is uploaded.

MOSH

Datamosh two video clips on your phone. Removes the keyframes so the second clip's motion drags the first clip's picture. Pads, effects and a saved bank.


ICARUS

A tilt-controlled flight game. Tilt to climb, roll to turn, fly toward the sun.


HANDSYNTH

A synth you play with your hands through the camera. Right hand sets pitch, left hand sets tone. The camera image stays on your device.

Free to use. Provided as-is.
