Manufacturing Standards

All units are manufactured to the following standards. Units failing any standard are destroyed, or sold at a discount.

STD-01CONTRAST. No midtones. Every unit is pushed until the grey is gone: either black and white, or black and one colour. A photograph with a full tonal range is asking you to believe it. A photograph with two tones is asking you to look at it. What survives is the unit. What does not was decoration.
STD-02SOURCE. Found, not shot. Tape, scans, magazines, television: material that already existed and already had a texture. The compression, the tracking lines, the dither and the moiré are the grain of the material and are not defects. The job is to find the image that is already in there and remove everything around it.
STD-03BODY. Mouths without faces. Hands without arms. A torso in liquid. A figure upside down. The body is cut where it needs to be cut, the way a collage cuts paper. No unit is about a person. Every unit is about the shape.
STD-04SOUND. Mean level −17 dB. No dynamics. No air. The pictures have no midtones and the sound has none either. Audio units that breathe are returned to the line.
STD-05EQUIPMENT. A phone. A desktop built from parts. Free software. No unit is generated. No unit is rendered on a farm.
STD-06PACKAGING. This portal. One file, written by hand, in the manner of portals that were written by one person for no reason except that they wanted to, which the customer could tell.
